Autosled Promotes Sam Goldenberg to Vice President of Dealer Sales ROCKVILLE, MD - September 24, 2024 - Autosled, No. 12 on the 2024 Inc. 5000 list of fastest-growing private companies, and a leading provider of digital technology solutions for automotive vehicle transport logistics, announced today the promotion of Sam Goldenberg to Vice President of Dealer Sales.
